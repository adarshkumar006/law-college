Welcome to Smt. Shanti Devi Vidhi Mahavidyalaya

Smt. Shanti Devi Vidhi Mahavidyalaya (College Code: 1434) is a distinguished institution dedicated to legal education, situated at Puramufti, Jagdishpur, Handia, Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh. The college is affiliated with Prof. Rajendra Singh (Rajju Bhaiya) University, Prayagraj and approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI), New Delhi.

Focused exclusively on legal studies through its 3-Year LL.B. degree program, the institution fosters intellectual acumen, statutory interpretation, courtroom advocacy, and high moral integrity. Students are provided with hands-on moot court exercises, legal aid outreach, and continuous guidance from experienced law teachers and legal luminaries.
